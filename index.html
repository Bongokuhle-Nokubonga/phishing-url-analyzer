    Got a suspicious link?

    1. Don't type passwords or banking details on a page you reached from a link. Open the site yourself.
    2. Banks and SARS will never ask for your PIN or OTP by email or SMS.
    3. At work, report it to your IT or security team, even if you already clicked.
